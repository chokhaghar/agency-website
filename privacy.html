Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or order, you share your name, project name, city and message with us through WhatsApp. You may also send video footage, logos, phone numbers and voice-overs for editing.

How we use it

  • Only to discuss, edit and deliver your videos.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • We never sell or share your details or footage with anyone.

Your footage

Your raw footage and final videos stay yours. We may show finished work in our portfolio only with your permission — ask us anytime to remove it.

Storage

Files are kept for up to 60 days after delivery for revisions, then deleted on request.

Website

This website does not use advertising cookies. Fonts and animation libraries load from Google Fonts and public CDNs.

Contact

For any privacy request, message us on WhatsApp or email us — details are on the contact section.
